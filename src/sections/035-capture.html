How it was recorded

  1. One step at a time

    An operator disassembles the machine by hand. Every step is logged: the action, the part and the tool.

  2. Four views after every step

    The scanner and three cameras record what is left on the bench, so all four views show the same state.

  3. Annotated backward

    Annotators start from the late steps: a part that is exposed there is followed back through the frames where it was covered, under one identity.

  4. Questions, video and robot data

    Question-answer pairs are generated from the annotations and the log. Two video cameras film the operator's hands, and a robot arm repeats part removals under teleoperation.
